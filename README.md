 # 🌍 Global Health & Socioeconomic Impact Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes global health outcomes using demographic, socioeconomic, and healthcare system data. The goal is to understand how factors like healthcare access, income, and education influence mortality and recovery rates across countries.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze global disease burden and trends
* Evaluate the relationship between healthcare access and mortality
* Assess the impact of socioeconomic factors on health outcomes
* Identify the most effective treatment methods

---

## 📁 Dataset

* **Size:** ~1,000,000 rows
* **Scope:** Multi-country, multi-year global health data
* **Key Features:**

  * Country
  * Year
  * Disease Category
  * Mortality Rate (%)
  * Recovery Rate (%)
  * Healthcare Access (%)
  * Doctors per 1000
  * Hospital Beds per 1000
  * Per Capita Income
  * Education Index
  * Population Affected

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

---

## 📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

* Corrected data types (numeric, percentage, categorical)
* Replaced incorrect aggregations (SUM → AVERAGE where needed)
* Ensured proper time-series sorting using Year
* Created calculated measures using DAX
* Structured data into a star schema model

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

### 🔹 Global Health Trends

* Recovery rate (~74.5%) is significantly higher than mortality (~5.05%)
* Disease burden is distributed across multiple categories

### 🔹 Healthcare System Analysis

* Higher healthcare access is associated with slightly lower mortality
* Healthcare infrastructure is relatively consistent across countries

### 🔹 Socioeconomic Impact

* Education shows a positive relationship with recovery rates
* Income has a weak correlation with mortality outcomes

---

## 💡 Key Business Recommendations

* Invest in preventive healthcare strategies
* Improve public health education
* Focus on disease-specific interventions
* Enhance treatment quality beyond infrastructure expansion

---

## 📌 Conclusion

The analysis shows that while global healthcare systems are effective overall, improvements in education, preventive care, and targeted treatment strategies are essential to further reduce mortality rates and improve outcomes.
